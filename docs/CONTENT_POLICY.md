# Content and distribution policy

MESUT PS4 STORE is a catalogue and package manager for homebrew and content that users are authorized to obtain.

## Allowed
- Open-source homebrew distributed under terms that permit redistribution.
- Developer tools and community utilities with explicit distribution permission.
- User-provided package URLs for content the user has the right to use.
- Catalogue metadata and artwork used with permission or under applicable terms.
- External links to a source's public homepage or public item page, without copying protected content.

## Not allowed
- Hosting, mirroring, indexing direct download links, or redistributing unauthorized commercial game packages.
- Circumventing DRM, authentication, subscriptions, paywalls, anti-bot protections, or other access controls.
- Scraping protected pages or automating a third-party site's download workflow.
- Presenting a third-party catalogue listing as proof that a package is legally distributable.
- Silently downloading or installing packages without user confirmation.

## Source review checklist
Before enabling an in-app package download, verify:
1. Who publishes the package?
2. Does the publisher explicitly permit redistribution or direct linking?
3. Is the URL canonical and served over HTTPS?
4. Is the package checksum available?
5. Are version and firmware requirements documented?
6. Are metadata and artwork licensed for the intended use?

If any material point is unknown, keep the entry informational and disable the package action.
