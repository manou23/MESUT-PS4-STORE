# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The MESUT PS4 STORE community aims to be welcoming, respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give actionable feedback and describe technical problems clearly.
- Welcome different experience levels and ask questions in good faith.
- Respect intellectual property, privacy, and platform safety.
- Keep discussions relevant and avoid harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, and personal attacks.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, doxxing, hate speech, intimidation, spam, malicious links, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Maintainer responsibility

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Reports should be made through the repository's available private contact or GitHub reporting tools.

This code applies to project spaces, issues, pull requests, and other community channels representing MESUT PS4 STORE.
